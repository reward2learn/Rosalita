## ARTICLE 2 — IN-KIND CAPITAL CONTRIBUTION (INBRENG)

### 2.1 Statutory Basis

2.1.1 The Parties expressly acknowledge and agree that this transaction is structured as an **in-kind capital contribution (setoran modal non-tunai / inbreng)** governed by **Article 34 of Law No. 40/2007 on Limited Liability Companies (UU PT)** , which provides:

> **Ayat (1):** *"Penyetoran atas modal saham dapat dilakukan dalam bentuk uang dan/atau dalam bentuk lainnya."*
> [Capital contributions may be made in cash and/or in other forms.]

> **Ayat (2):** *"Penyetoran sebagaimana dimaksud pada ayat (1) yang dilakukan dalam bentuk lainnya sebagaimana dimaksud dalam Pasal 33 ayat (1) huruf b, harus dinilai dengan harga wajar yang ditetapkan oleh ahli yang tidak terafiliasi dengan Perseroan."*
> [Contributions as referred to in paragraph (1) made in other forms as referred to in Article 33 paragraph (1) letter b, must be valued at fair market value determined by an expert not affiliated with the Company.]

> **Ayat (3):** *"Ketentuan lebih lanjut mengenai penyetoran dalam bentuk lainnya sebagaimana dimaksud pada ayat (1) diatur dengan Peraturan Pemerintah."*
> [Further provisions regarding contributions in other forms as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be regulated by Government Regulation.]

2.1.2 The Parties further acknowledge that the inbreng mechanism is implemented in accordance with **Government Regulation No. 43/2011 on Procedures for Submission and Valuation of In-Kind Capital Contributions**.

### 2.2 Nature of Contribution

2.2.1 Bristow (the "**Contributor**") hereby agrees to contribute, transfer, assign, and convey to Tokenizin, all of Bristow's right, title, and interest in and to the Red Ruby Shares, free and clear of all Encumbrances and third-party claims whatsoever, as an in-kind capital contribution (inbreng) to Tokenizin's capital.

2.2.2 The Red Ruby Shares constitute "other forms" (bentuk lainnya) of capital contribution under Article 34(1) of UU PT, being intangible movable assets (benda bergerak tidak berwujud) that are capable of being valued in money.

2.2.3 Upon Closing, legal and beneficial ownership of the Red Ruby Shares shall pass from Bristow to Tokenizin, and Red Ruby shall become a direct subsidiary of Tokenizin.

### 2.3 Legal Form Requirements

2.3.1 The Inbreng shall be effected through the following notarial deeds executed simultaneously:

| No | Deed / Akta | Purpose | Legal Basis |
|----|-------------|---------|-------------|
| 1 | **Akta Pernyataan Keputusan RUPS Tokenizin** | Recording the RUPS resolution approving: (a) Capital Increase; (b) Inbreng from Bristow; (c) Issuance of New Tokenizin Shares; (d) AD/ART amendment | Pasal 39-44, 87-91 UU PT |
| 2 | **Akta Perubahan Anggaran Dasar Tokenizin** | Amending Tokenizin's AD/ART to reflect increased authorized/capital and new shareholder | Pasal 7-18 UU PT |
| 3 | **Akta Setoran Modal Non-Tunai (Akta Inbreng)** | Recording Bristow's in-kind contribution of Red Ruby Shares to Tokenizin, with Independent Valuation attached | Pasal 34 UU PT, PP 43/2011 |
| 4 | **Akta Pengalihan Saham** | Transferring the Red Ruby Shares from Bristow to Tokenizin in the records of Red Ruby | Pasal 56 UU PT |

2.3.2 All deeds shall be executed in the **Indonesian language** before a licensed Notaris and shall comply with all applicable provisions of:
   - (a) UU PT, particularly Articles 34, 39-44, 50, 56-61;
   - (b) Law No. 2/2014 on Notary Positions (UU Jabatan Notaris);
   - (c) PP No. 43/2011 on Procedures for Submission and Valuation of In-Kind Capital Contributions;
   - (d) The AD/ART of Tokenizin and Red Ruby.

### 2.4 Prohibition on Nominee Arrangements

2.4.1 The Parties expressly declare, acknowledge, and agree that this transaction:

   - (a) Does **NOT** constitute a nominee arrangement, "pinjam nama" agreement, or any arrangement prohibited under **Article 33 of Law No. 25/2007 on Investment (UU PM)** ;
   - (b) Is **NOT** intended to hold shares for and on behalf of any other person;
   - (c) Shall result in **Tokenizin being registered as the sole legal and beneficial owner** of the Red Ruby Shares in its own name in Red Ruby's DPS.

2.4.2 The Parties acknowledge that **Article 33 paragraphs (1) and (2) of UU PM** provide:

   > *(1) Penanam modal dalam negeri dan penanam modal asing yang melakukan penanaman modal dalam bentuk perseroan terbatas dilarang membuat perjanjian dan/atau pernyataan yang menegaskan bahwa kepemilikan saham dalam perseroan terbatas dimiliki untuk dan atas nama orang lain.*
   > *(2) Perjanjian dan/atau pernyataan sebagaimana dimaksud pada ayat (1) dinyatakan batal demi hukum.*

2.4.3 Tokenizin represents and warrants that it acquires the Red Ruby Shares for its own account and benefit, and not as nominee, trustee, representative, or agent for any other person.

### 2.5 Clear Title to Red Ruby Shares

2.5.1 Bristow represents and warrants that at all times from the Effective Date through Closing:
   - (a) He is the sole legal and beneficial owner of the Red Ruby Shares;
   - (b) The Red Ruby Shares are free and clear of all mortgages, liens, pledges, encumbrances, claims, equities, and third-party rights;
   - (c) He holds the Red Ruby Shares in his own name, not as nominee for any other person;
   - (d) There are no pending or threatened legal proceedings affecting his title to the Red Ruby Shares.

2.5.2 Bristow acknowledges that the Inbreng constitutes a final and irrevocable transfer of all his rights as a direct shareholder in Red Ruby to Tokenizin. Upon Closing, Bristow shall have no further direct ownership interest in Red Ruby, and all his rights as a shareholder of Red Ruby (including voting rights, dividend rights, and inspection rights) shall be exercised solely by Tokenizin.

## ARTICLE 3 — TOKENIZIN SHARE ISSUANCE TO CONTRIBUTOR

### 3.1 Capital Increase

3.1.1 Concurrently with the Inbreng, Tokenizin shall increase its authorized, issued, and paid-up capital in accordance with the resolution of the RUPS to be recorded in the Akta Pernyataan Keputusan RUPS.

3.1.2 The Capital Increase shall be implemented in accordance with:
   - (a) **Article 39 of UU PT** — which permits capital increases through the issuance of new shares;
   - (b) **Article 40 of UU PT** — which requires RUPS approval for capital increases;
   - (c) **Article 41 of UU PT** — which requires notification and registration of capital changes;
   - (d) The AD/ART of Tokenizin.

### 3.2 Issuance of New Tokenizin Shares

3.2.1 In consideration for the Inbreng of the Red Ruby Shares (as valued under Article 4), Tokenizin shall issue to Bristow the New Tokenizin Shares, calculated as follows:

   **Number of New Tokenizin Shares = Value of Red Ruby Shares ÷ Nominal Value per Tokenizin Share** *

   * As determined by the Independent Valuer and approved by RUPS.

3.2.2 The New Tokenizin Shares shall:
   - (a) Be of the same class and series as existing Tokenizin shares (or such other class as may be determined by RUPS);
   - (b) Carry full voting, dividend, and distribution rights from the date of issuance;
   - (c) Be fully paid-up upon completion of the Inbreng;
   - (d) Be registered in Bristow's name in Tokenizin's DPS.

### 3.4 Pre-Emptive Rights (HMETD)

3.4.1 The Parties acknowledge that under **Article 43 of UU PT**, Existing Tokenizin Shareholders have pre-emptive rights (HMETD) to subscribe for new shares in proportion to their existing ownership before such shares may be offered to a non-shareholder (Bristow).

3.4.2 The Existing Tokenizin Shareholders, by executing this Deed or a separate written instrument attached as **Schedule D**, hereby:
   - (a) Acknowledge receipt of the formal HMETD offer from Tokenizin;
   - (b) Waive their pre-emptive rights in respect of the New Tokenizin Shares to be issued to Bristow;
   - (c) Consent to the issuance of the New Tokenizin Shares to Bristow;
   - (d) Confirm that such waiver is made voluntarily and with full understanding of their rights under Article 43 of UU PT.

3.4.3 If the AD/ART of Tokenizin requires a higher quorum or special resolution for the waiver of pre-emptive rights, such requirements shall be satisfied prior to Closing.

### 3.5 Restrictions on Transfer of New Tokenizin Shares

3.5.1 Bristow agrees that the New Tokenizin Shares shall be subject to the following transfer restrictions:

   | Restriction | Period | Detail |
   |-------------|--------|--------|
   | **Lock-Up** | ________ (___) months from Closing | Bristow may not sell, transfer, pledge, or encumber the New Tokenizin Shares without prior written consent of Tokenizin's Board of Directors |
   | **Right of First Refusal (ROFR)** | ________ (___) months following Lock-Up | Before transferring to any third party, Bristow must first offer the shares to Existing Tokenizin Shareholders on the same terms |
   | **Tag-Along Rights** | Perpetual | If Existing Tokenizin Shareholders sell their shares to a third party, Bristow has the right to join the sale on proportionate terms |
   | **Drag-Along Rights** | Perpetual | If Existing Tokenizin Shareholders holding >___% of Tokenizin shares sell to a bona fide third party, Bristow must join the sale on proportionate terms |

3.5.2 These restrictions shall be recorded in Tokenizin's AD/ART and/or a separate Shareholders' Agreement.

3.5.3 The Parties acknowledge that the purpose of these restrictions is to ensure alignment of interests and to prevent Bristow from immediately liquidating his Tokenizin position, thereby defeating the alignment purpose of the inbreng structure.

### 3.6 Bristow's Rights as Tokenizin Shareholder

3.6.1 As a holder of New Tokenizin Shares, Bristow shall have all statutory rights of a shareholder under UU PT, including:
   - (a) Voting rights at RUPS (Article 52 UU PT);

   - (c) Inspection rights (Articles 75-76 UU PT);
   - (d) Right to request a Special Audit (Articles 110-113 UU PT);
   - (e) Right to sue the Company and/or Management (Article 61 UU PT, Article 1365 KUHPerdata).

3.6.2 Bristow's exercise of these rights shall be subject to:
   - (a) The AD/ART of Tokenizin;
   - (b) The provisions of this Deed;
   - (c) Good faith and the principles of GCG.

3.6.3 **Governance Commitment**: Bristow acknowledges and agrees that as a Tokenizin shareholder, he shall:
   - (a) Vote in good faith at RUPS;
   - (b) Not obstruct, delay, or frustrate Tokenizin's management of Red Ruby as its subsidiary;
   - (c) Cooperate in any governance reforms proposed by Tokenizin majority shareholders;
   - (d) Not use his Tokenizin shareholding to unduly influence Red Ruby's operations in a manner inconsistent with Tokenizin's business judgment.

## ARTICLE 4 — VALUATION AND EXCHANGE RATIO

### 4.1 Independent Valuation Requirement

4.1.1 The Parties shall jointly appoint a licensed **Independent Valuer (KJPP)** to determine:
   - (a) The fair market value of the Red Ruby Shares (the "**Contribution Value**");
   - (b) The fair market value of Tokenizin shares (the "**Tokenizin Share Value**");
   - (c) The exchange ratio for the New Tokenizin Shares to be issued to Bristow.

4.1.2 The Independent Valuer shall be:
   - (a) Registered with the OJK and the Indonesian Institute of Public Accountants (IAPI);
   - (b) Independent and not affiliated with any Party;
   - (c) Appointed by mutual written agreement of the Parties.

4.1.3 The valuation shall be conducted in accordance with:
   - (a) **Indonesian Valuation Standards (SPI)** ;
   - (b) **International Valuation Standards (IVS)** ;
   - (c) **Government Regulation No. 43/2011** on procedures for in-kind capital contribution valuation;
   - (d) Applicable regulations of the Indonesian Institute of Public Accountants (IAPI).

### 4.2 Valuation Methodology

4.2.1 The valuation of the Red Ruby Shares shall consider, at minimum:
   - (a) The audited financial statements of Red Ruby;
   - (b) The asset value, earnings, and cash flow of Red Ruby;
   - (c) Comparable market transactions and industry multiples;
   - (d) The goodwill, brand value, and license value of Red Ruby;
   - (e) Any liabilities, contingent or otherwise, of Red Ruby;
   - (f) The outlook and prospects of Red Ruby's business.

4.2.2 The valuation of Tokenizin shares shall consider, at minimum:
   - (a) Tokenizin's financial position and assets;
   - (b) Tokenizin's intellectual property, technology platform, and business value;
   - (c) Tokenizin's revenue, growth prospects, and comparable valuations.

### 4.3 Exchange Ratio and Share Calculation

The exchange ratio and number of New Tokenizin Shares shall be determined as follows:

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Fair Market Value of Red Ruby Shares (A) | IDR _______________ |
| Fair Market Value per Existing Tokenizin Share (B) | IDR _______________ |
| Exchange Ratio (A ÷ B) | _______________ : 1 |
| Number of New Tokenizin Shares to be issued to Bristow (A ÷ B) | _______________ shares |
| Bristow's resulting ownership percentage in Tokenizin | _______________ % |

### 4.4 Valuation Report

4.4.1 The Independent Valuer shall prepare a written valuation report (the "**Valuation Report**") containing:
   - (a) Detailed valuation methodology and assumptions;
   - (b) Supporting financial analysis;
   - (c) Final valuation conclusions;
   - (d) The Valuer's certification of independence.

4.4.2 The Valuation Report shall be completed and delivered to all Parties no later than _______________ (__) days before the anticipated Closing Date.

4.4.3 The costs of the Independent Valuer shall be borne by Tokenizin, with each Party bearing its own internal costs.

### 4.5 Legal Effect of Valuation

4.5.1 The Valuation Report shall form the basis for:
   - (a) The number of New Tokenizin Shares to be issued to Bristow;
   - (b) The RUPS resolution approving the Capital Increase;
   - (c) The AD/ART amendment recording the share issuance;
   - (d) Any tax filings related to the Inbreng.

4.5.2 All Parties agree that the valuation shall be final and binding for purposes of this Deed, absent manifest error or fraud.

4.5.3 The Valuation Report shall be attached to the Akta Setoran Modal Non-Tunai (Akta Inbreng) as required by **Article 34(2) of UU PT** and **PP 43/2011**.
