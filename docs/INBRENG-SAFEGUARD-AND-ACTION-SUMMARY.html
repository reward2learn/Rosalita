9. COMPARISON — WHICH STRATEGY TO CHOOSE?

Choose DIRECT PURCHASE (existing document) if:

  • Tokenizin has adequate cash and can pay IDR to Bristow
  • Tokenizin does NOT want Bristow as a shareholder
  • Speed is a priority (fewer approvals needed)
  • Simpler structure is preferred
  • Bristow wants immediate cash liquidity

Choose INBRENG (this alternative deed) if:

  • Tokenizin wants to conserve cash
  • Tokenizin wants Bristow aligned as an equity stakeholder
  • Bristow is willing to accept Tokenizin shares instead of cash
  • Existing Tokenizin shareholders agree to dilution
  • Both parties want potential tax-neutral treatment
  • A longer timeline is acceptable

HYBRID option (not yet drafted — request if desired):

  • Direct purchase for control + warrant/reinvestment option giving Bristow the right to buy Tokenizin shares with proceeds

10. CRITICAL WARNING ABOUT BRISTOW AS TOKENIZIN SHAREHOLDER

**This structure makes Bristow a shareholder of Tokenizin.** If Tokenizin has concerns about Bristow's governance:

✅ **Article 9.8** contains a legally-binding Governance Commitment where Bristow agrees:

  • Not to interfere with Tokenizin's management of Red Ruby
  • To raise concerns only through internal governance channels
  • That breach can trigger mandatory buyback of his shares

✅ **Lock-up provisions (Article 3.5)** prevent Bristow from immediately selling his Tokenizin shares.

✅ **Drag-along rights** ensure Bristow cannot block a future sale of Tokenizin.

⚠ **However**, Bristow will have voting rights, inspection rights, and RUPS attendance rights as a Tokenizin shareholder. Tokenizin should carefully evaluate whether this governance dynamic is acceptable before proceeding with this structure.
