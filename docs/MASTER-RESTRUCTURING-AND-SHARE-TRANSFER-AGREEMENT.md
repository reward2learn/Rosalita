## ARTICLE 3: MANAGEMENT ACCOUNTABILITY AND REFORM

### 3.1 Statutory Basis for Management Accountability

3.1.1 The Parties acknowledge and agree that **Articles 85, 97, and 98 of UU PT** establish the legal framework for Director and Commissioner accountability:

   - **Article 85**: Directors manage the Company in accordance with its purpose and objectives
   - **Article 97(1)**: Directors are responsible for the management of the Company
   - **Article 97(2)**: Directors must manage the Company in good faith and with full responsibility
   - **Article 97(3)**: Directors are personally liable for losses suffered by the Company if they are proven to have acted in bad faith or negligence
   - **Article 98**: Commissioners supervise Directors and may temporarily suspend Directors
   - **Article 114(4)**: Commissioners are liable for losses if found negligent in their supervisory duties

3.1.2 Tokenizin, as a shareholder, has the statutory right to hold Management accountable under **Articles 75, 76, 79, 85, 97, and 98 of UU PT**, as well as under the general principles of **Pasal 1365 KUHPerdata** (unlawful act / perbuatan melawan hukum) and **Pasal 1243 KUHPerdata** (damages for breach / wanprestasi).

### 3.2 Management Accountability Mechanisms

3.2.1 **Shareholder Inspection Rights** (Pasal 75-76 UU PT)

   Tokenizin shall have the right at all times to:
   - (a) Examine all books, records, financial statements, contracts, and documents of Red Ruby;
   - (b) Request and receive copies of all company records;
   - (c) Request information from the Board of Directors on any matter concerning the Company;
   - (d) The Company shall respond to any written request from Tokenizin within fourteen (14) calendar days.

3.2.2 **Right to Request Special Audit** (Pasal 110-113 UU PT)

   (a) Tokenizin, as a shareholder holding at least one-tenth (1/10) of the total shares with voting rights, shall have the right to request a **Special Audit** of Red Ruby if there are reasonable grounds to suspect:
      - (i) Unlawful conduct by Management causing loss to the Company;
      - (ii) Breach of fiduciary duty by Management;
      - (iii) Misappropriation or misuse of Company assets;
      - (iv) Gross negligence in management resulting in operational or financial losses.

   (b) The Special Audit shall be conducted by an independent auditor appointed by Tokenizin and approved by the Pengadilan Negeri (District Court), in accordance with Article 112 UU PT.

   (c) The cost of the Special Audit shall be borne by Red Ruby unless the audit reveals wrongdoing by Management, in which case the cost shall be recoverable from the responsible Directors/Commissioners.

   (d) Tokenizin may apply to the Pengadilan Negeri for a Special Audit order under **Pasal 111 UU PT**, and the Court may grant the request upon a prima facie showing of justifiable cause.

3.2.3 **Right to Convene RUPS** (Pasal 79 UU PT)

   (a) Tokenizin shall have the right to request that the Board of Directors convene a RUPS for any lawful purpose, including:
      - (i) Replacement of Directors or Commissioners;
      - (ii) Amendment of AD/ART;
      - (iii) Approval of material transactions;
      - (iv) Discussion of audit findings;
      - (v) Any other matter within the authority of the RUPS.

   (b) If the Directors fail to convene a RUPS within fifteen (15) days of Tokenizin's written request, Tokenizin may convene the RUPS itself at the Company's expense, in accordance with **Pasal 79(4) UU PT**.

   (c) Tokenizin may also request the Pengadilan Negeri to order the convening of a RUPS if the Directors fail to do so or if the Company has no Directors, under **Pasal 79(4)-(5) UU PT**.

### 3.3 Operational Negligence — Definition and Consequences

3.3.1 For purposes of this Agreement, "**Operational Negligence**" includes, but is not limited to:

   | Category | Examples |
   |----------|----------|
   | **Financial Mismanagement** | Failure to maintain proper books and records; misappropriation of funds; unauthorized expenditure; failure to file tax returns; non-payment of statutory obligations |
   | **Operational Inefficiency** | Failure to implement proper internal controls; sustained negative cash flow due to poor management decisions; failure to maintain required licenses and permits |
   | **Regulatory Non-Compliance** | Violation of applicable laws including labor law, tax law, licensing requirements, health and safety regulations, PDP law |
   | **Breach of Fiduciary Duty** | Self-dealing; conflicts of interest not disclosed; unauthorized contracts favoring related parties; failure to act in the Company's best interests |
   | **Asset Mismanagement** | Misuse, waste, or unauthorized disposal of Company assets; failure to maintain physical assets |
   | **Record-Keeping Failures** | Incomplete, inaccurate, or false record-keeping; failure to maintain statutory registers (DPS, Daftar Pemilik Manfaat, etc.) |

3.3.2 Upon Tokenizin's written notice identifying specific instances of Operational Negligence with supporting evidence:
   - (a) The Board of Directors shall respond in writing within fourteen (14) days;
   - (b) If the response is insufficient or the negligence is confirmed, Tokenizin may exercise its rights under Article 3.2 and 3.4;
   - (c) Directors found liable for Operational Negligence under **Pasal 97(3) UU PT** shall be personally liable for losses suffered by the Company.

### 3.4 Right to Reform and Replace Management

3.4.1 **Right to Replace Directors and Commissioners** (Pasal 105-108 UU PT)

   (a) Tokenizin, exercising its voting rights as a shareholder, shall have the right to vote for the appointment, removal, and replacement of members of the Board of Directors and Board of Commissioners.

   (b) Removal of a Director or Commissioner may be effected at any time by a resolution of the RUPS, in accordance with the AD/ART and **Pasal 105(1) UU PT**.

   (c) A Director may be removed by the RUPS even without stated cause, provided that the removal is decided at a RUPS and the Director concerned is given the opportunity to defend himself/herself, in accordance with **Pasal 105(2) UU PT**.

   (d) If removal is for cause (including Operational Negligence), the Director shall not be entitled to any compensation or severance beyond statutory minimums.

3.4.2 **Right to Suspend Directors** (Pasal 98 UU PT)

   (a) The Board of Commissioners may temporarily suspend a Director for justifiable cause, including suspected Operational Negligence.

   (b) Tokenizin may request the Board of Commissioners to exercise this suspension power.

   (c) If the Board of Commissioners fails to act within a reasonable time, Tokenizin may convene a RUPS to address the matter directly.

3.4.3 **Right to Reform Governance Structure**

   (a) Tokenizin shall have the right to propose and vote on amendments to the AD/ART to implement Good Corporate Governance (GCG) reforms, including but not limited to:
      - (i) Changes to the composition or qualifications of the Board of Directors and Board of Commissioners;
      - (ii) Implementation of internal audit and compliance functions;
      - (iii) Adoption of a code of conduct and ethics policy;
      - (iv) Adoption of a related-party transaction policy;
      - (v) Adoption of a whistleblower policy;
      - (vi) Appointment of a corporate secretary.

   (b) Such amendments, if approved by the RUPS, shall be submitted to the Menkumham for approval in accordance with the procedures of UU PT.

3.4.4 **Right to Appoint Operational Supervisor**

   Tokenizin shall have the right to appoint one or more operational supervisors or advisors to monitor day-to-day operations and report to Tokenizin. Such supervisors shall have reasonable access to all Company premises, records, and personnel.
