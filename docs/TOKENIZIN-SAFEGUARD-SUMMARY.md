## 2. MANAGEMENT ACCOUNTABILITY PROTECTIONS

| Your Right | How It Works | Legal Backing |
|-----------|-------------|---------------|
| **Inspect all company records** | Right to examine books, financials, contracts at any time | Pasal 75-76 UU PT |
| **Request Special Audit** | If you suspect mismanagement, request court-ordered independent audit at company expense | Pasal 110-113 UU PT |
| **Convene RUPS** | Force a shareholder meeting if Directors refuse; or get court order | Pasal 79 UU PT |
| **Remove Directors** | Vote them out at RUPS, with or without cause (if without cause, they get notice and chance to defend) | Pasal 105 UU PT |

## 3. OPERATIONAL NEGLIGENCE — DEFINED & ACTIONABLE

The Agreement defines **Operational Negligence** as including:

- Financial mismanagement (improper books, unauthorized spending, tax non-compliance)
- Operational inefficiency (poor controls, sustained losses, missing licenses)
- Regulatory non-compliance (labor, health, safety, PDP law violations)
- Breach of fiduciary duty (self-dealing, undisclosed conflicts)
- Asset mismanagement (misuse, waste, unauthorized disposal)
- Record-keeping failures (incomplete statutory registers)

**Action Plan if Negligence is Found:**
1. You send written notice with evidence
2. Directors have 14 days to respond
3. If insufficient → you exercise Special Audit (Pasal 110-113)
4. If confirmed → you convene RUPS to remove/replace Directors
5. Directors personally liable for losses (Pasal 97(3))
