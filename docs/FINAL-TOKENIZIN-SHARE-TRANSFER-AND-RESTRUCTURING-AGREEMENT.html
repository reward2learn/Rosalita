ARTICLE 2 — TRANSFER OF SHARES / PENGALIHAN SAHAM

2.1 Agreement to Transfer

2.1.1 The Sellers (Bristow and Starworks, jointly and severally) hereby agree to sell, transfer, assign, and convey to Tokenizin, and Tokenizin hereby agrees to purchase, acquire, and take transfer from the Sellers, all of the Sellers' right, title, and interest in and to the Shares, free and clear of all Encumbrances and third-party claims whatsoever.

2.1.2 The legal and beneficial ownership of the Shares shall pass to Tokenizin upon:

  • (a) The execution of the Akta Jual Beli Saham before the Notaris;
  • (b) Payment of the Purchase Price in full;
  • (c) The registration of Tokenizin in the DPS of Red Ruby.

2.2 Legal Form Requirements

2.2.1 The Parties expressly acknowledge and agree that the Share Transfer shall be effected exclusively through an Akta Jual Beli Saham executed before a licensed Notaris in the Indonesian language, as mandated by Article 56 paragraph (1) of Law No. 40/2007 (UU PT) which provides:

"Pemindahan hak atas saham dilakukan dengan akta pemindahan hak." (Transfer of rights over shares shall be effected by a deed of transfer of rights.)

2.2.2 The Akta Jual Beli Saham shall comply with all applicable provisions of:

  • (a) UU PT, particularly Articles 56-61;
  • (b) Law No. 2/2014 on Notary Positions (UU Jabatan Notaris);
  • (c) The AD/ART of Red Ruby;
  • (d) All other applicable laws and regulations.

2.2.3 The Akta Jual Beli Saham shall contain, at minimum:

  • (a) The full identity of each Seller and the precise number and class of shares transferred by each;
  • (b) The full identity of Tokenizin as the Buyer;
  • (c) The Purchase Price in Indonesian Rupiah (IDR);
  • (d) The representations and warranties set forth in Article 6;
  • (e) The signatures of all Parties or their lawful attorneys-in-fact;
  • (f) The Notaris's attestation and seal.

2.3 Prohibition on Nominee Arrangements

2.3.1 The Parties hereby expressly declare, acknowledge, and agree that this Agreement and the Akta Jual Beli Saham:

  • (a) Do NOT constitute a nominee arrangement, "pinjam nama" agreement, or any arrangement prohibited under Article 33 of Law No. 25/2007 on Investment (UU PM) ;
  • (b) Are NOT intended to hold shares for and on behalf of any other person;
  • (c) Shall result in Tokenizin being registered as the sole legal and beneficial owner of the Shares in its own name in the DPS.

2.3.2 The Parties acknowledge that Article 33 paragraphs (1) and (2) of UU PM provide:

(1) Penanam modal dalam negeri dan penanam modal asing yang melakukan penanaman modal dalam bentuk perseroan terbatas dilarang membuat perjanjian dan/atau pernyataan yang menegaskan bahwa kepemilikan saham dalam perseroan terbatas dimiliki untuk dan atas nama orang lain. (2) Perjanjian dan/atau pernyataan sebagaimana dimaksud pada ayat (1) dinyatakan batal demi hukum.

[(1) Domestic and foreign investors establishing a limited liability company are prohibited from making any agreement or statement declaring that share ownership in the company is held for and on behalf of another person. (2) Any such agreement or statement as referred to in paragraph (1) is declared null and void by law.]

2.3.3 Tokenizin represents and warrants that it acquires the Shares for its own account and benefit, and not as nominee, trustee, representative, or agent for any other natural or legal person.

2.3.4 Any provision of this Agreement or any related document that may be construed as contrary to Article 33 of UU PM shall be deemed automatically severed and void, and the remaining provisions shall continue in full force and effect.

2.3.5 The Parties further acknowledge that Article 48 paragraph (1) of UU PT requires that shares be issued in the name of their owner:

"Saham Perseroan dikeluarkan atas nama pemiliknya." (Company shares shall be issued in the name of their owner.)

2.4 Pre-Emptive Rights and Shareholder Approvals

2.4.1 The Sellers represent and warrant that they have obtained, prior to the execution of this Agreement:

  • (a) A formal written waiver of pre-emptive rights (penawaran hak memesan efek terlebih dahulu) from the Co-Shareholder, in compliance with Article 43 of UU PT, confirming that the Co-Shareholder does not wish to purchase the Shares and consents to the transfer to a third party;
  • (b) If required by the AD/ART, a valid RUPS resolution approving the transfer of Shares to Tokenizin, in compliance with Articles 57 and 87-91 of UU PT.

2.4.2 If the AD/ART does not require RUPS approval for the transfer, the Sellers shall provide a written legal opinion from the Notaris confirming this fact.

2.4.3 The Co-Shareholder, by executing this Agreement, acknowledges and confirms:

  • (a) Receipt of the mandatory pre-emptive rights offer;
  • (b) Waiver of such pre-emptive rights;
  • (c) Consent to the transfer of Shares to Tokenizin;
  • (d) Agreement to cooperate in updating the DPS and other corporate records.

2.5 Proceeds of Crime and AML Compliance

2.5.1 Each Party represents and warrants that the funds, assets, and consideration used in connection with this Agreement are derived from lawful sources and do not constitute proceeds of crime as defined under Law No. 8/2010 on the Prevention and Eradication of Money Laundering (UU TPPU) .

2.5.2 Each Party shall:

  • (a) Provide KYC (Know Your Customer) documentation as reasonably requested;

8.3 Operational Negligence — Definition

For the purposes of this Agreement, "Operational Negligence" means any act or omission by Management that results in or could reasonably be expected to result in loss, damage, or harm to Red Ruby, including but not limited to:

Category Specific Acts or Omissions
Financial Mismanagement (a) Failure to maintain proper books, accounts, and financial records; (b) Misappropriation, misapplication, or unauthorized use of Company funds; (c) Unauthorized expenditure exceeding limits set by the AD/ART or RUPS; (d) Failure to file tax returns or pay taxes due; (e) Failure to remit statutory contributions (BPJS, etc.); (f) Incurring debt without proper authorization; (g) Making loans to related parties without RUPS approval.
Operational Inefficiency (a) Sustained negative cash flow or trading losses due to demonstrably poor management decisions; (b) Failure to implement adequate internal controls; (c) Failure to maintain required business licenses, permits, or registrations; (d) Failure to maintain adequate insurance coverage; (e) Gross inefficiency in operations resulting in material financial loss.
Regulatory Non-Compliance (a) Violation of labor laws (UU Ketenagakerjaan); (b) Violation of health, safety, or hygiene regulations; (c) Violation of alcoholic beverage licensing laws; (d) Violation of applicable tourism/hospitality regulations; (e) Violation of data protection laws (UU PDP); (f) Failure to report to or cooperate with regulatory authorities.
Breach of Fiduciary Duty (a) Self-dealing or transactions with related parties not at arm's length; (b) Conflicts of interest not disclosed to the Board or RUPS; (c) Entering into contracts that favor a related party at the Company's expense; (d) Misuse of corporate opportunities; (e) Misuse or abuse of corporate assets or intellectual property.
Asset Mismanagement (a) Misuse, waste, or unauthorized disposal of Company assets; (b) Failure to maintain physical assets, premises, or equipment; (c) Allowing assets to deteriorate or lose value through neglect; (d) Unauthorized creation of liens or encumbrances.
Record-Keeping Failures (a) Failure to maintain the DPS as required by Article 50 UU PT; (b) Failure to maintain the Daftar Pemilik Manfaat as required by PP 13/2018; (c) Failure to prepare and present annual financial statements; (d) Failure to maintain proper RUPS and Board meeting minutes; (e) Inaccurate, incomplete, or falsified records.

8.4 Procedure for Addressing Operational Negligence

If Tokenizin has reasonable grounds to believe that Operational Negligence has occurred, the following procedures shall apply:

Step 1: Written Notice Tokenizin shall deliver a written notice to the Board of Directors specifically identifying the alleged Operational Negligence and providing supporting evidence.

Step 2: Management Response Within fourteen (14) calendar days of receipt of the notice, the Board of Directors shall deliver a written response:

  • (a) Admitting the Operational Negligence and stating remedial measures;
  • (b) Denying the Operational Negligence with supporting evidence; or
  • (c) Requesting additional time to investigate (not to exceed an additional fourteen (14) days).

Step 3: Investigation If Tokenizin is not satisfied with the Management response, Tokenizin may:

  • (a) Exercise its inspection rights under Article 8.2.1;
  • (b) Request a Special Audit under Article 9;
  • (c) Convene a RUPS under Article 8.2.2.

Step 4: Remedial Action If Operational Negligence is confirmed:

  • (a) Tokenizin may convene a RUPS to remove the responsible Director(s) or Commissioner(s);
  • (b) Tokenizin may initiate legal proceedings for personal liability under Article 97(3) of UU PT;
  • (c) The Company may seek damages from the responsible Director(s) or Commissioner(s) under Article 97(3) UU PT and/or Article 1365 KUHPerdata (unlawful act).

13.3.4 The Parties expressly waive any right to appeal the arbitration award to the extent permitted by applicable law.
